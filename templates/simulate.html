Parameter Meaning & Abbreviations

  • UAV: Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (airborne edge server)
  • CPU (GHz): Computation speed (more GHz = faster processing)
  • κ (Kappa): Energy efficiency constant (lower = better battery life)
  • Task Size (MB): Input data processed locally or offloaded
  • Cycles: Required processing effort for completing task
  • Bandwidth: Speed of wireless communication link
  • Battery (J): UAV energy storage capacity in Joules
